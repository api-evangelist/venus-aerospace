## Products

- [Rotating Detonation Rocket Engine (RDRE)](https://www.venusaero.com/rdre): Detonation-based rocket engine using supersonic detonation waves around an annular combustor instead of conventional subsonic combustion; no moving parts, standard materials, positioned for defense munitions and uncrewed systems, space upper stages, orbital transfer and landers.
- [Venus Detonation Ramjet (VDR)](https://www.venusaero.com/vdr): Hybrid engine pairing the RDRE with air-breathing ramjet technology to power a vehicle from takeoff to hypersonic cruise on a single engine.
